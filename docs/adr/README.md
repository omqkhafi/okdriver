## Template

```md
# NNN. Title

## Status
Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNN

## Context
What problem or force makes this decision necessary?

## Decision
What we decided, stated plainly.

## Consequences
What becomes easier, harder or different as a result.

## Evidence
Benchmarks, tests, specs or prior art that support the decision.
```
